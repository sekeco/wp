Eight ways to switch the theme

Each page is the same Sekeco landing with a working toggle and a GSAP transition. Open one, try it in both directions, and pick. Sizes and positions are sketches; the idea is what counts.

Round one

A

Horizon

A sunrise-to-night track in the header. The orb is draggable and its position is the theme: drag it and the whole page cross-fades with it, release and it snaps to the nearest side.

Toggle lives in the header. Scrubbable, no jump cut. Slightly wider than an icon. B

Pull cord

A beaded cord hangs from the top edge. Pull it and let go: the cord recoils on an elastic, the room flickers and the lights switch.

Most playful. Desktop first; tap works on touch. Needs the top-right free. C

Blinds

An icon on the left edge. Nine shutters close across the page in a stagger, the word Day or Night passes, and the shutters open on the new theme.

Strongest moment. Hides the swap completely, so it is robust. Longest animation. D

Lens

The icon above search. Hold it and a lens of the other theme opens; drag it around to peek, drop it back on the button to cancel, release elsewhere to switch for good.

Most novel. A tap still switches. Needs a hint the first time.

Round two

E

Sunrise

An icon in the header. The new theme rises from the bottom as a curved horizon with a warm glow on its edge when going to light, and a cool one going to dark.

Cinematic and readable. Icon-only, so it keeps the header calm. Glow is the heavy part. F

Divider

A grip peeks from the left edge. Drag it across the page like a before and after slider: the new theme sits behind the line. Past a third it commits, otherwise it springs back.

Lets you preview without committing. Not icon-only: the grip is a visible tab. G

Dial

A rotary knob above search with three detents: Light, Auto, Dark. Turn it and the page dims continuously; release and it clicks to the nearest detent. A tap cycles.

The only one that offers Auto (follow the system). Best if you want a real preference. H

Cascade

An icon at the lower right. Each section re-themes in turn from the one on screen down the page, as a wave, each with a small lift.

Gentle and layout-aware. No snapshot trick, so nothing can go stale. Slower to finish on long pages.

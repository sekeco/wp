## Scope

Mode: Persuade (home, services, contact) with Experience passes on Works and Portfolio archives. Whole-site replacement of the Sekeco block theme and plugin component blocks. Visitor: a business owner or product lead deciding whether to start a project.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The work is the page. Oversized type and rounded project surfaces carry the site; the visitor sees real Works within the first scroll. Refuses the category default: centered headline, two buttons, a row of equal icon cards, glow gradients on near-black.

OWN-WORLD: Deep blue-black ink #07070D and lavender paper #F6F5FB (dark first), the logo gradient (blue #4D68E4, purple #A33FBD, red-pink #FD065A, cyan glint #09C4F7) on buttons, pills, cover cards and the closing field, Bricolage Grotesque headings with DM Sans text, 20px surfaces, pointer-lit neon cards (ring plus soft glow under the cursor), a vertical side rail with the Dark/Light switch.

STORY: Sekeco takes a brand or an operational system from identity to running software with one team. The visitor sees real works, understands the four capability lines, and starts an enquiry through the form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width floating pill header (hamburger circle, wordmark, centred inline menu, sage Start a project pill), small uppercase label then a centred three-line headline at about 4.5rem with its closing words dimmed, and a 8/4 then 4/4/4 mosaic of real Works entering below. Rail on the left edge.

FORM: Pinned by the user: design system of Ohio demo 8 (warm dark/light, rounded cards, type-led sections, overlaid project titles), reinterpreted in Sekeco's own palette and type. No catalog roll was run (skipped because the user pinned the direction). seed: none (roll skipped)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature interaction and motion grammar

GSAP: headline lines rise from a mask on load; project cards parallax their images and swell on hover while the title lifts over the image; stat counters tick once; sections reveal with one exponential ease-out; cross-document view transitions between pages. Reduced motion shows everything static.

## Unresolved

Works have no featured images locally: the typographic cover is the designed answer, owner can add photos later. Client logos, testimonials and metrics are intentionally absent.
